Lattice Strain Heatmap

Ba (2.15 Å) and K (2.03 Å) push the lattice outward; P (1.07 Å) and S (1.04 Å) pull it tight. The resulting internal tension scatters dislocations — cracks can't propagate through the chaos.

Neon Cage & Radiation Hit

Ne atoms sit in interstitial voids, caged by surrounding metal atoms. Click ☢️ Fire Radiation — the streak hits the Ne bubble, which absorbs the impact and vibrates harder, protecting the rest of the structure.

⚡

Pm Beta Emission

Every few seconds, a Pm atom fires a beta particle (β⁻ electron) that bounces through the lattice until captured by P or As collector atoms — turning the crystal into a self-powered micro-circuit.
